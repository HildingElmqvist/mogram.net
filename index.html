Mogram AB is a software company in Lund, Sweden founded in January 2016. The focus is software for modeling and simulation.

Current activities include designing and implementing a Modelica translator and simulator running in your browser. There are two web apps:

  • Modelica Instant Simulator: modiator.com
  • Modelica Instant Calculator: modicalc.com

CEO of Mogram is Dr Hilding Elmqvist.
